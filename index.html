Why Civic Participation Matters

Shape Policy

Your participation directly influences the policies that govern our communities and nation.

Strengthen Democracy

An active citizenry is the cornerstone of a robust and representative democratic system.

Community Impact

Engaging in civic life helps address local issues and build stronger, more vibrant communities.

How to Participate in Elections

1

Register to Vote

Ensure you are registered. Check your status and register online or at designated centers.

2

Find Your Polling Place

Locate your assigned polling station and its operating hours before election day.

3

Understand the Candidates

Research candidates and their platforms to make informed decisions. Focus on issues that matter to you.

4

Cast Your Vote

On election day, visit your polling place and exercise your right to vote.
